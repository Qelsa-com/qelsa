## User-facing copy

Keep candidate and employer UI copy tight. Say more with fewer words:

- One short sentence or a 1–2 line summary per card, section, or modal.
- Do not stack explanatory paragraphs on a page; move detail into the flow, a tooltip, or admin docs.
- Avoid repeating the same idea in a heading, subtitle, and helper text.
- Direct labels over descriptions. If the control explains itself, do not add another sentence.
- Keep AI-generated summaries short (about 120 characters for cards, 240 for descriptions).
